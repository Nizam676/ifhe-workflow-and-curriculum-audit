## 7. Method and limitations

1. Use the original employer career page where available.
2. Record the job title, employer, location, publication date, URL, and access date.
3. Extract explicit requirements rather than relying only on job-title wording.
4. Separate essential requirements from preferred qualifications.
5. Record experience and degree requirements.
6. Map each skill to an official course and learning outcome.
7. Avoid treating three postings as representative of every employer or role.
8. Recheck posting availability before submission.
