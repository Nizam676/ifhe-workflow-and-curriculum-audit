# Task 2: Curriculum Audit and Redesign

## 1. Project overview

This project audits the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering curriculum at ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education (IFHE), Hyderabad, with a focus on the curriculum applicable to the 2023–2027 admission batch.

The purpose is to compare the existing curriculum with relevant industry skill requirements and propose evidence-based improvements while maintaining academic fundamentals, prerequisite relationships, credit balance, and applicable regulations.

This is an independent student investigation and redesign proposal. It does not represent an officially approved revision of the IFHE curriculum.

## 2. Objectives

- Map courses and credits by semester using the applicable official syllabus.
- Evaluate existing courses using KEEP, UPDATE, REDUCE, and ADD categories.
- Compare curriculum coverage with at least three real, dated job descriptions.
- Document five AI-assisted exercises across five subjects, including prompts, model details, outputs, and independent verification.
- Propose changes affecting approximately 20–25% of the total programme credits.
- Explain prerequisites, credit adjustments, regulatory conditions, and implementation limitations.
- Develop the Task 1 campus maintenance workflow into a proposed lab or capstone project.
- Include an interactive course comparison or credit calculator.
- Seek voluntary feedback from a faculty member or graduate, or document why feedback could not be obtained.

## 4. Methodology

The investigation follows these steps:

1. Identify the official syllabus and regulations applicable to the programme and admission batch.
2. Extract course titles, course codes, semesters, and credits.
3. Review job descriptions and identify recurring technical and professional skills.
4. Map identified skills to curriculum courses and learning outcomes.
5. Evaluate courses and document reasons for each recommendation.
6. Design a credit-balanced proposal and check prerequisite dependencies.
7. document AI-assisted exercises and verify their outputs independently.
8. Develop an applied lab or capstone based on the Task 1 workflow investigation.
9. Record feedback, evidence, assumptions, and limitations.

## 5. Evidence and integrity

Official curriculum documents should take priority over general programme webpages. Industry requirements should be supported by actual job postings with publication dates and URLs.

Credit values, course details, test results, AI outputs, and stakeholder feedback must not be invented. Any information that could not be verified will be identified as pending, assumed, illustrative, or unavailable.

## 6. Expected outcome

The final outcome is a documented curriculum audit, a proposed redesign, a traceable evidence register, five verified AI-assisted exercises, and a working interactive credit-planning tool.

The proposal is intended for academic discussion. Any formal curriculum change would require review and approval by the relevant university authorities.
