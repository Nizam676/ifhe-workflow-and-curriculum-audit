# Official Curriculum Map

## 1. Purpose

This document records the existing B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering curriculum applicable to the IFHE Hyderabad 2023–2027 admission batch. It establishes the baseline against which proposed curriculum changes will be evaluated.

## 2. Official curriculum source

- Institution: ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education, Hyderabad
- Programme: B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering
- Admission batch: 2023–2027
- Curriculum version: To be confirmed from the applicable official document
- Official syllabus/regulations title: To be entered after verification
- Document URL or student-portal reference: To be recorded
- Publication or revision date: To be recorded
- Date accessed: 24 October 2026
- Verification status: Pending confirmation of the batch-specific document

The official programme structure webpage may be used as a starting point, but it must not be treated as proof that its current curriculum matches the 2023 admission batch.

## 5. Course categories

Classify each course using the official course structure where possible:

- Mathematics and basic sciences
- Engineering and computing foundations
- Core computer science
- Programme electives
- Laboratories and practical courses
- Projects, internships, and capstones
- Humanities, communication, and professional development
- Other official categories

Do not reclassify a compulsory course as an elective merely because a proposed redesign would benefit from more flexibility.

## 6. Verification procedure

1. Confirm that the source applies to the correct programme and admission batch.
2. Record each course code and title exactly as published.
3. Record credits from the official document.
4. Compare the extracted course list with official semester totals, if provided.
5. Recalculate semester and programme totals independently.
6. Record discrepancies rather than silently correcting them.
7. Link the source document in `evidence/sources.md`.

## 7. Limitation

The mapping is not complete until the applicable official syllabus and regulations have been verified. Recommendations that depend on unverified course names, credits, or prerequisites must remain provisional.
