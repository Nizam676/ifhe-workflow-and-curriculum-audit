# Feedback, Limitations, and Future Work

## 1. Purpose

This document records stakeholder feedback, limitations in the evidence, and areas that require further validation before the proposed curriculum redesign could be considered for formal academic review.

## 2. Feedback plan

The project seeks voluntary feedback from one or more of the following:

- A faculty member familiar with the B.Tech CSE curriculum.
- A graduate of the same or a closely related programme.
- A professional working in software development, data analysis, or a relevant technical role.

Participation is voluntary. No person should be identified publicly without their permission.

## 5. Limitations

### Curriculum version

The audit depends on identifying the exact syllabus and regulations applicable to the 2023–2027 admission batch. A general or newer programme webpage may not reflect the applicable curriculum.

### Job-description sample

Three job descriptions provide a limited sample. Requirements vary by employer, role, location, seniority, and sector. This analysis should not be interpreted as a comprehensive labour-market survey.

### Experience level

Some selected job postings may request specialist skills or professional experience. Their requirements are useful as examples of industry practice but should not automatically be treated as minimum expectations for fresh graduates.

### Course mapping

A course title alone does not establish the extent of topic coverage. A defensible mapping requires the syllabus, learning outcomes, teaching activities, and assessment methods.

### AI-assisted exercises

AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate, or dependent on the prompt and model version. Findings are valid only when the actual outputs and independent verification steps are recorded.

### Credit estimates

Credit-impact calculations remain provisional until the official total credits, semester distribution, and programme rules are verified.

### Capstone feasibility

A workflow capstone may require supervision, appropriate data, secure test environments, and suitable assessment arrangements. The proposal does not establish that the university has approved or scheduled such a project.

## 6. Ethical and academic considerations

- Use only sources that can be identified and checked.
- Do not invent course details, employer requirements, test results, or feedback.
- Do not publish personal, confidential, or sensitive institutional information without authorisation.
- Use synthetic or anonymised data for workflow demonstrations.
- Record AI assistance transparently.
- Distinguish current curriculum facts from proposed future changes.

## 7. Future work

1. Obtain and verify the applicable official syllabus and regulations.
2. Complete the semester-wise course and credit mapping.
3. Expand the job-description sample if time permits.
4. Conduct voluntary stakeholder feedback.
5. Test the interactive calculator with verified credit values.
6. Review the proposal for course duplication, prerequisites, and semester workload.
7. Revise the proposal after new evidence becomes available.

## 8. Final statement

This project is an evidence-informed student audit and redesign proposal. It does not claim official curriculum approval, completed stakeholder consultation, or validated implementation outcomes unless such evidence is explicitly recorded.
