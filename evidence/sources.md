## 4. Source-handling rules

1. Prefer primary sources, including official university documents and employer career pages.
2. Preserve the original URLs and publication dates.
3. Record the access date.
4. Do not copy more material than needed to substantiate a claim.
5. Separate directly observed facts from interpretations.
6. Mark inaccessible, outdated, or unverified sources.
7. Recheck URLs before final submission.

## 5. Citation convention

Use short in-text source identifiers, such as `[C1, p. 12]` or `[J2]`, and ensure each identifier refers to a record above. Replace page numbers with the actual page where the claim appears.
